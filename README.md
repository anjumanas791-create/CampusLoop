# CampusLoop

A campus-only exchange where verified students buy, sell and donate used items.

**Product Management Minor Project** | Cluelearn Nationwide Industrial Training and Internship Program  
**Submission date:** 10 November 2026

---

## About This Project

CampusLoop is a product concept developed as a product management project. It takes a real user problem through the full product lifecycle: discovery, definition, design, planning, launch and measurement.

This is **not a coding project**. There is no application source code in this repository. The deliverables are product management artifacts: research, a PRD, a prioritized backlog, wireframes, a clickable prototype, a roadmap, a metrics plan and a go-to-market plan.

## The Problem

Students buy textbooks, electronics and room essentials, then stop needing them. There is no easy, trusted way to pass these items to other students on campus, so many are kept idle or thrown away. Students also told us they lack a reliable platform to buy second-hand items in good condition without worrying about fraud, and that they don't know where to sell their notes and used study material online.

## The Solution

CampusLoop is a campus-only exchange for verified students.

**MVP features:** verified sign-up, listings with a donate option, search and filters, item details, contact with the seller, marking items as sold or given, and reporting listings.

**Later releases:** in-app chat, saved items and meetup suggestions (V2), then an impact tracker and seller ratings (V3).

## Key Research Findings

The research included an anonymous survey of **13 students** and **two student interviews**.

- 67% of respondents with unused items kept them idle or threw them away.
- None of the 13 respondents use any platform to buy or sell used items.
- Finding reliable buyers or sellers was the top problem (33%).
- Student ID verification was the top trust feature (54%).
- 92% want to buy, sell and donate in one place.
- Interest in a campus-only app was mixed (average 3.0 out of 5).

**Limitation:** The sample is small and mostly first-year students, so findings are indicative, not conclusive.

## Methods and Tools

**Research:** anonymous Google Forms survey and two student interviews. **Prioritization:** RICE framework, cross-checked with MoSCoW. **Design:** low-fidelity wireframes and a clickable Figma prototype. **Documents:** PRD, report, abstract and deck. **Presentation:** Google Slides.

## Privacy and Ethics

- The survey was anonymous, and no personal data was collected.
- No personal data, API keys, passwords or secrets are included in this repository.
- Competitor information comes from public sources listed in the project report's references.
- The design avoids dark patterns and misleading claims.
